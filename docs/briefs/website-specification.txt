<quality_assurance>
Before finalizing, verify:

RESEARCH
- Every board-specific component claim is sourced or marked unverified.
- Uno Rev3 is not confused with Uno R4 or clones.
- Power-selection circuitry is correctly identified.
- MOSFET type and part number are verified before being stated.
- Regulator identities are verified.
- Clock-device types are verified.
- No process node is invented.
- No transistor count is invented.

EDUCATIONAL ACCURACY
- Pure silicon is correctly described as a semiconductor.
- Doping modifies semiconductor carrier concentration.
- N-type and P-type are not described as globally charged chunks of material.
- Holes are explained as effective positive charge carriers.
- Transistors are controlled through electric fields.
- Resistors are not described as semiconductors by definition.
- Capacitors are not described as storing charge inside the dielectric.
