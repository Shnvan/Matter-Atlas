# Atoms, electrons, nuclei and electrical effects

## Atom model

Atoms contain a nucleus and electrons described by quantum states/probability distributions. Proton charge is positive, electron negative, neutron has no net charge; atomic number counts protons, and a neutral atom has equal proton/electron counts. Use boron/silicon/phosphorus/copper as relevant examples after verifying specific element numbers. Hydrogen probability imagery helps explain clouds but is not an exact multi-electron silicon orbital calculation. [SRC-022](../../SOURCES.md)

No planetary trajectories. If a Bohr-style historical comparison is included, label it “Simplified historical model — electrons do not literally orbit like planets.” Cloud density should communicate probability rather than a time exposure of a tiny planet.

## Cross-cutting electrical explanation

Charge is an electric property; voltage is potential difference/energy per charge; current is the rate of net charge flow. Electric field affects charges. Resistance relates voltage/current under stated conditions and transfers energy to material; capacitance relates separated electrode charge to potential difference. Teach these from every scale node without pretending every electrical behavior is a literal mechanical analogy. [SRC-020, SRC-021](../../SOURCES.md)

Conduction electrons have net drift superimposed on microscopic motion. An electrical disturbance propagates much faster than net drift; no fixed fraction of light speed applies without a specific structure/source. Ordinary circuits transfer energy while charge is conserved; electrons are not fuel consumed by a resistor or LED. Current markers use conventional direction unless electron motion is explicitly labeled.

## Nucleus and optional bonus

Nucleus -> protons/neutrons is a composition branch. Optional quarks/gluons sit beyond normal electronics instruction. Electrons and photons are elementary in the Standard Model; protons/neutrons are composite. Mark limits of present theory and that gravity is not part of that model. [SRC-024](../../SOURCES.md)

## Scene bounds

Use conceptual clouds and schematic nuclei, source any sizes, and omit electron “radius” guesses. Provide readable static equivalents and reduced-motion support. See [photons](photons.md), [terminology](../product/terminology.md), and [model contract](../../CONTENT_MODEL.md).
